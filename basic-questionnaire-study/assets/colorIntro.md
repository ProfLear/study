# Color vision assessment

In what follows, you will be asked to answer questions regarding a series of color images. There is no right or wrong answer. We simply what to assess your color vision, before we perform the ordering tasks. There will be 4 questions. For each question, you will be shown a circle of dots, and asked to identify the number inside the circle. You can also indicate that you do not know what number is present.
