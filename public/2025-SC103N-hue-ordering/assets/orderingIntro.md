# The Ordering Task

Welcome to this study on perceptions of ordering. In the survey that follows, you will be shown two images and asked to select the image that you think has the better ordering. Each question will always be organized so that there is an upper and lower image that you are asked to compare.

In some cases, the ordering may be obvious. **For instance, in the example below most people would likely identify the top image as better ordered. In the example, this option is selected.**
